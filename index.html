07 / Origin Story

The Builder's Journey

My journey into tech began with hardware, building an Arduino-based railway crossing system in high school. This early fascination with making things work evolved into a deep interest in software and algorithms, leading me to place 6th out of 14,000+ participants in the GeeksForGeeks Coding Cup.

I then transitioned into research, publishing work on Computer Vision with the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) and the Semiconductor Laboratory. This foundation allowed me to scale my work into production environments, deploying real-time edge AI models across hundreds of cameras for massive-scale events.

Today, while pursuing my Master's at McMaster University, I focus on the cutting edge of enterprise AI. From building mortality forecasting models and knowledge graphs at Manulife, to engineering scalable multi-agent platforms at TCS, my goal is to bridge the gap between advanced research and robust, production-ready data science workflows.
